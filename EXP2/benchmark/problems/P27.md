# P27 — seven_customer_symmetric_tsp_mtz

## Problem description

A traveling salesman must visit 7 customers at 7 locations, starting and ending at location 1. The symmetric distances are: d12=86, d13=49, d14=57, d15=31, d16=69, d17=50, d23=68, d24=79, d25=93, d26=24, d27=5, d34=16, d35=7, d36=72, d37=67, d45=90, d46=69, d47=1, d56=86, d57=59, d67=81. Formulate a mathematical program to determine the visiting order minimizing total travel distance.
