# P40 — three_task_method_selection_worker_mix_fixed_setup_mip

## Problem description

A company requires skilled workers and laborers for three tasks. It must choose exactly one method for each task. Task 1 requires 8400 effective hours and may use method A (skilled workers alone) or method B (groups of one skilled worker and two laborers, with a fixed weekly setup cost of 500 yuan). Task 2 requires 10800 effective hours and may use skilled workers alone or laborers alone. Task 3 requires 18000 effective hours and may use groups of five laborers or groups of one skilled worker and three laborers. Skilled workers earn 100 yuan per week and work 42 effective hours; laborers earn 80 yuan per week and work 36 effective hours. At most 400 skilled workers and 800 laborers can be hired. If task 1 uses method B, task 3 cannot use the pure-labor method. If task 3 uses its mixed method, at least 20 skilled workers must be assigned. Total skilled workers cannot exceed 60% of total laborers. Establish a mathematical model minimizing weekly wages plus fixed costs.
