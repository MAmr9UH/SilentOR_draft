# P31 — jieli_branch_professional_goal_planning_preference_city

## Problem description

Jieli Company needs to recruit three types of professionals to work in two regional branches located in Donghai City and Nanjiang City. The demand for different professionals in these regional branches is shown in Table 4-3. The company has categorized applicants into six types. Each type has a number of people, suitable specialties, a preferred specialty, and a preferred city as shown in Table 4-4. The company uses lexicographic goal programming priorities: P1 fully meet all specialty demands at both branches; P2 maximize the number of recruited personnel assigned to their preferred specialty, with a target of 8000 if possible; P3 maximize the number assigned to their preferred city, with a target of 8000 if possible. Under these priorities, determine at least how many people cannot meet P3.

Table 4-3: Demand by branch and specialty: Donghai specialty 1: 1000, Donghai specialty 2: 2000, Donghai specialty 3: 1500, Nanjiang specialty 1: 2000, Nanjiang specialty 2: 1000, Nanjiang specialty 3: 1000.

Table 4-4: Type 1 has 1500 people, suitable specialties 1 and 2, preferred specialty 1, preferred city Donghai. Type 2 has 1500 people, suitable specialties 2 and 3, preferred specialty 2, preferred city Donghai. Type 3 has 1500 people, suitable specialties 1 and 3, preferred specialty 1, preferred city Nanjiang. Type 4 has 1500 people, suitable specialties 1 and 3, preferred specialty 3, preferred city Nanjiang. Type 5 has 1500 people, suitable specialties 2 and 3, preferred specialty 3, preferred city Donghai. Type 6 has 1500 people, suitable specialty 3, preferred specialty 3, preferred city Nanjiang.
