# P10 — gandhi_cloth_fixed_charge

## Problem description

Gandhi Cloth can make shirts, shorts, and pants. Making each type requires renting the appropriate machinery weekly: shirt machinery $200, shorts machinery $150, pants machinery $100. Labor and cloth per unit: shirt 3 labor-hours and 4 sq yd cloth; shorts 2 and 3; pants 6 and 4. Each week 150 labor-hours and 160 sq yd of cloth are available. Selling price and variable cost per unit: shirt sells $12 costs $6; shorts $8 and $4; pants $15 and $8. Machinery for a clothing type must be rented if any of that type is produced. Maximize weekly profit (contribution minus rental).
