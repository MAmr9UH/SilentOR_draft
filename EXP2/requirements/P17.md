# P17 requirements — logisticorp_facility_location_7x9

## Problem description

LogistiCorp is at a pivotal juncture, faced with the challenge of efficiently supplying nine retail stores from a pool of seven possible distribution centers. Each center presents a unique opening cost and has a specific capacity limit. Moreover, transporting goods from these centers to the retail stores incurs varying costs. The aim is to fulfill the demand at each retail store while minimizing the overall cost, which encompasses the opening expenses of the distribution centers and the transportation costs of the goods.

## R_demand · demand_satisfaction

For each store s in {s1, s2, s3, s4, s5, s6, s7, s8, s9}, the total shipments delivered to that store, which is the sum of `f_c_s` over all centers c in {c1, c2, c3, c4, c5, c6, c7}, must be >= that store's demand. The demands for stores {s1, s2, s3, s4, s5, s6, s7, s8, s9} are 695, 737, 452, 616, 587, 779, 892, 440, 556 respectively.

- Source status: STATED
- Ambiguity flag: false

## R_capacity_open_link · capacity_linking

For each center c in {c1, c2, c3, c4, c5, c6, c7}, the total outbound shipments from that center, which is the sum of `f_c_s` over all stores s in {s1, s2, s3, s4, s5, s6, s7, s8, s9}, must be <= that center's capacity multiplied by its opening variable `y_c`. The capacities for centers {c1, c2, c3, c4, c5, c6, c7} are 814, 864, 1320, 1952, 1447, 1886, 1962 respectively. This forces all shipments out of center c to be 0 when `y_c` = 0.
