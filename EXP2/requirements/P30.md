# P30 requirements — three_year_investment_cash_flow_lp

## Problem description

Someone has a fund of 300,000 yuan and has the following investment projects in the next three years:
(1) Investment can be made at the beginning of each year within three years, with an annual profit of 20% of the investment amount, and the principal and interest can be used for investment in the following year;
(2) Investment is only allowed at the beginning of the first year, and it can be recovered at the end of the second year, with the total principal and interest amounting to 150% of the investment amount, but the investment limit is no more than 150,000 yuan;
(3) Investment is allowed at the beginning of the second year within three years, and it can be recovered at the end of the third year, with the total principal and interest amounting to 160% of the investment amount, and the investment limit is 200,000 yuan;
(4) Investment is allowed at the beginning of the third year within three years, and it can be recovered in one year with a profit of 40%, and the investment limit is 100,000 yuan.
Try to determine an investment plan for this person that maximizes the principal and interest at the end of the third year.

## Candidate-facing variable meanings

- `x_annual_y1` — amount invested at beginning of year 1 in the one-year 20 percent annual project
- `x_annual_y2` — amount invested at beginning of year 2 in the one-year 20 percent annual project
- `x_annual_y3` — amount invested at beginning of year 3 in the one-year 20 percent annual project
- `x_project2_y1` — amount invested at beginning of year 1 in the two-year project returning 150 percent at end of year 2
- `x_project3_y2` — amount invested at beginning of year 2 in the project returning 160 percent at end of year 3
- `x_project4_y3` — amount invested at beginning of year 3 in the one-year 40 percent profit project
- `cash_after_y1` — uninvested cash carried after year-1 investment decisions
- `cash_after_y2` — uninvested cash carried after year-2 investment decisions
- `cash_after_y3` — uninvested cash carried after year-3 investment decisions
- `final_amount` — principal and interest available at the end of year 3

## R_year1_budget · flow_balance

At the beginning of year 1, the sum of `x_annual_y1`, `x_project2_y1`, and `cash_after_y1` must be exactly equal to the initial fund of 300000 yuan.

## R_year3_budget · flow_balance

At the beginning of year 3, the sum of `x_annual_y3`, `x_project4_y3`, and `cash_after_y3` must be exactly equal to `cash_after_y2` plus 1.2 times `x_annual_y2`, which is the year-2 annual-project investment returned at 120 percent.

## R_final_balance · flow_balance
