# P38 requirements — two_product_july_december_inventory_production_storage_lp

## Problem description

A factory must schedule production for products I and II from July to December. Product I demand is 30000 units per month from July to September and 100000 units per month from October to December. Product II demand is 15000 units per month from July to September and 50000 units per month from October to December. From June to December, production costs are 4.50 yuan per unit for product I and 7 yuan per unit for product II. The combined production capacity for both products is 120000 units per month. Product I occupies 0.2 cubic meters per unit in inventory and product II occupies 0.4 cubic meters per unit. The factory's own warehouse capacity is 15000 cubic meters. Own warehouse storage costs 1 yuan per cubic meter per month, and external rented storage costs 1.5 yuan per cubic meter per month. Initial inventory of both products at the beginning of July is zero. Determine the July-December production and inventory plan that minimizes total production and inventory cost while meeting all monthly demand.
