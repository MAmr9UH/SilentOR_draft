# P43 requirements — person_project_hour_assignment_integer_min_cost

## Problem description

Assign each person's available hours to projects at minimum total cost. Every person's assigned hours must equal that person's supply, every project's received hours must equal its demand, and each person-project assignment must respect its contribution limit. Assigned hours are nonnegative integers. Use the supplied supply, demand, cost, and limit matrices.
