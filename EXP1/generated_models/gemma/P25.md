# Gemma 3 12B - P25 - `vrphtw_20_customer_euclidean_distance`

## Full problem description

Global Logistics must serve 20 customers from depot 0 using at most 5 trucks of capacity 200. Each truck starts and returns to the depot. Every customer must be served exactly once. Customer demands, coordinates, hard time windows, and a fixed 90-minute service duration are given. Early arrivals may wait, but service must begin within the customer time window. The objective is to minimize total Euclidean travel distance. Depot 0 is at (40,50) with time window [0,1236]. Customer data: 1 (45,68), demand 10, [912,967]; 2 (45,70), demand 30, [825,870]; 3 (42,66), demand 10, [65,146]; 4 (42,68), demand 10, [727,782]; 5 (42,65), demand 10, [15,67]; 6 (40,69), demand 20, [621,702]; 7 (40,66), demand 20, [170,225]; 8 (38,68), demand 20, [255,324]; 9 (38,70), demand 10, [534,605]; 10 (35,66), demand 10, [357,410]; 11 (35,69), demand 10, [448,505]; 12 (25,85), demand 20, [652,721]; 13 (22,75), demand 30, [30,92]; 14 (22,85), demand 10, [567,620]; 15 (20,80), demand 40, [384,429]; 16 (20,85), demand 40, [475,528]; 17 (18,75), demand 20, [99,148]; 18 (15,75), demand 20, [179,254]; 19 (15,80), demand 10, [278,345]; 20 (30,50), demand 10, [10,73]. Provide an operations research model for this VRP with hard time windows and solve for minimum total distance.
