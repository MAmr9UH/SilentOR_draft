# Llama 3.3 70B - P41 - `two_fertilizer_machine_inventory_max_ending_lp`

## Full problem description

A company operates two machines to produce two fertilizers: liquid and solid. One lot of liquid requires 50 minutes on Machine 1 and 30 minutes on Machine 2. One lot of solid requires 24 minutes on Machine 1 and 33 minutes on Machine 2. At the beginning of the week, inventories are 30 lots of liquid and 90 lots of solid. Machine 1 has 40 available hours and Machine 2 has 35 available hours. Forecast demand is 75 lots of liquid and 95 lots of solid. The company aims to maximize the total ending inventory of liquid and solid. Formulate a linear program.
