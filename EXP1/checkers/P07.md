# P07 — `multiproduct_production_inventory` checker documentation

## Full problem description

A factory produces three products I, II, III to meet contracted quarterly orders over four quarters. Orders (units) — product I: 1500, 1000, 2000, 1200 in quarters 1-4; product II: 1500, 1500, 1200, 1500; product III: 1000, 2000, 1500, 2500. There is no inventory at the start of quarter 1, and 150 units of each product must be in inventory at the end of quarter 4. The factory has 15,000 hours of production time per quarter; producing one unit of I, II, III takes 2, 4, 3 hours respectively. Because of equipment replacement, product I cannot be produced in quarter 2. If a product is not delivered on time, a late penalty of $20 per unit per quarter applies to products I and II and $10 per unit per quarter to product III. Products that are produced but not delivered in the same quarter incur a storage cost of $5 per unit per quarter. Arrange production to minimize the total late-penalty and storage cost.
