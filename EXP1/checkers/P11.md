# P11 — `food_manufacture_blending` checker documentation

## Full problem description

A food company plans buying, storage, and blending of five raw oils over six months (January-June) to maximize profit. Vegetable oils VEG1, VEG2 and non-vegetable oils OIL1, OIL2, OIL3 are refined on separate lines: at most 200 tons of vegetable oil and 250 tons of non-vegetable oil can be refined per month. Refining has no weight loss and no cost. The final product sells for 150 pounds/ton. Oil purchase prices per ton by month (Jan-Jun): VEG1 110,130,110,120,100,90; VEG2 120,130,140,110,120,100; OIL1 130,110,130,120,150,140; OIL2 110,90,100,120,110,80; OIL3 115,115,95,125,105,135. Up to 1000 tons of each raw oil may be stored at 5 pounds/ton/month; the final product and refined oils cannot be stored. The product hardness must be between 3 and 6 and blends linearly; hardness of VEG1 8.8, VEG2 6.1, OIL1 2.0, OIL2 4.2, OIL3 5.0. There are 500 tons of each raw oil in store at the start of January, and 500 tons of each must remain at the end of June. Additional restrictions: (1) at most three oils may be used in any month; (2) if an oil is used in a month, at least 20 tons of it must be used; (3) if VEG1 or VEG2 is used in a month, then OIL3 must also be used that month. Determine the buying, storage, usage, and production plan maximizing total profit.
