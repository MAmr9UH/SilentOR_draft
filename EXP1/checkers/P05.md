# P05 — `basketweavers_course_selection` checker documentation

## Full problem description

To graduate from Basketweavers University with a major in operations research, a student must complete at least two math courses, at least two OR courses, and at least two computer courses. Some courses count toward more than one requirement: Calculus fulfills the math requirement; operations research fulfills math and OR; data structures fulfills computer and math; business statistics fulfills math and OR; computer simulation fulfills OR and computer; introduction to computer programming fulfills computer; and forecasting fulfills OR and math. Some courses are prerequisites for others: Calculus is a prerequisite for business statistics; introduction to computer programming is a prerequisite for computer simulation and for data structures; and business statistics is a prerequisite for forecasting. Minimize the number of courses needed to satisfy the major requirements.
