# P02 - `or_course_selection` - EXP1 results

## Full problem description

A master's student majoring in Operations Research must take two math courses, two operations research courses, and two computer courses from seven courses: calculus, operations research, data structures, management statistics, computer simulation, computer programming, and forecasting. Calculus belongs to the math category, and computer programming belongs to the computer category. Operations research belongs to both operations research and math. Data structures belongs to both computer and math. Management statistics belongs to both math and operations research. Computer simulation belongs to both computer and operations research. Forecasting belongs to both operations research and math. For courses that belong to two categories, taking the course can count as taking one course in each category. Prerequisites: computer simulation or data structures must be taken after computer programming, management statistics must be taken after calculus, and forecasting must be taken after management statistics. Determine the minimum number of courses and which courses should be taken to meet the requirements.
