# P04 - `diet_min_cost` - EXP1 results

## Full problem description

You need to meet daily nutritional needs at minimum cost using five food options. Chicken provides 18 grams of protein, 6 grams of carbohydrates, and 142 calories at a cost of $4. Rice provides 12 grams of protein, 28 grams of carbohydrates, and 88 calories at a cost of $8. Broccoli provides 13 grams of protein, 19 grams of carbohydrates, and 137 calories at a cost of $7. Tofu provides 5 grams of protein, 16 grams of carbohydrates, and 141 calories at a cost of $10. Beans provides 10 grams of protein, 20 grams of carbohydrates, and 150 calories at a cost of $1. Daily nutritional targets are at least 56 grams of protein, 194 grams of carbohydrates, and 2012 calories. Determine the minimal cost to meet the daily nutritional requirements.
