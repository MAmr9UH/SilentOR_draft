# P21 - `post_office_5on2off_staffing` - EXP1 results

## Full problem description

The number of employees needed in a post office varies depending on the day of the week: Monday 15, Tuesday 13, Wednesday 15, Thursday 18, Friday 14, Saturday 16, and Sunday 10. Employees work five consecutive days and then have the next two days off. Organize groups of employees according to the day on which they start their five-day work stretch so that staffing requirements are satisfied every day of the week. Minimize the total number of workers employed.
