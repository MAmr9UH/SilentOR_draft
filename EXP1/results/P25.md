## Full problem description

Global Logistics must serve 20 customers from depot 0 using at most 5 trucks of capacity 200. Each truck starts and returns to the depot. Every customer must be served exactly once. Customer demands, coordinates, hard time windows, and a fixed 90-minute service duration are given. Early arrivals may wait, but service must begin within the customer time window. The objective is to minimize total Euclidean travel distance. Depot 0 is at (40,50) with time window [0,1236].
