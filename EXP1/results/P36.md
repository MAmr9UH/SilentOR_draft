# P36 - `warehouse_contract_covering_binary_logic_mip` - EXP1 results

## Full problem description

A factory must rent warehouse space for the next 4 months. The required warehouse area (in square meters) is given in Table 1-14: Month 1: 1500, Month 2: 1000, Month 3: 2000, Month 4: 1200. The factory can sign rental contracts of different lengths, with longer contracts offering discounted rates. The rental fee per 100 square meters is: 1-month contract 4000 yuan, 2-month contract 7500 yuan, 3-month contract 10500 yuan, 4-month contract 13000 yuan. Each contract, once chosen, must cover consecutive months starting from the beginning of the period. Additional business rules: the company may rent multiple warehouses in parallel; each warehouse contract must fully cover the selected months; the available capacity of each warehouse is unlimited; at least two different contract lengths must be signed over the 4 months; if a 4-month contract is chosen, then no 1-month contract may be chosen; the number of distinct warehouse contract lengths signed cannot exceed 3; and the demand for each month must be fully satisfied without shortage or excess. Integer variables represent how many contracts of each feasible start month and length are signed, and binary variables encode which contract lengths are used. Formulate this as a MILP model and find the minimum total rental cost.
