# P24 - `container_packing_minimum_count_with_requirements` - EXP1 results

## Full problem description

A company must pack goods into containers for transportation. Each container can hold at most 60 tons and each used container must load at least 18 tons. Goods A, B, C, D, and E have quantities 120, 90, 300, 90, and 120, and weights 0.5, 1.0, 0.4, 0.6, and 0.65 tons per unit. Whenever any A goods are loaded in a container, at least one unit of C must also be loaded in that container. Loading C alone does not require A. Each used container must load at least 12 units of D. Establish an operations research model that uses the fewest containers to pack all goods.
