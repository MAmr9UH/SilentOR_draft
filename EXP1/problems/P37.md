# P37 — `grain_trading_three_month_inventory_cash_flow_lp`

## Full problem description

A trading company specializes in wholesale grain. It has warehouse capacity of 5000 dan. On January 1 it has 1000 dan of grain in stock and 20000 yuan in cash. Estimated purchase and selling prices for months 1, 2, and 3 are: purchase prices 2.85, 3.05, and 2.90 yuan per dan; selling prices 3.10, 3.25, and 2.95 yuan per dan. Purchased grain is delivered in the same month but can only be sold in the next month, and payment is required upon delivery. The company wants to have exactly 2000 dan in inventory at the end of the quarter. Determine a purchasing and selling strategy that maximizes total profit over the three months.
