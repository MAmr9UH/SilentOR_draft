# P33 — `three_year_investment_cash_flow_lp_variant_500k`

## Full problem description

You have an initial capital of 500,000 yuan at the beginning of Year 1. Over the next three years, you may allocate funds to investment projects. Project 1 is a 1-year product available at the beginning of each year in Years 1-3; if you invest I at the start of a year, you receive 1.20 I at that year's end, with unlimited capacity. Project 2 is a 2-year product available only at the beginning of Year 1; it matures at the end of Year 2 and pays 1.50 I, with an investment cap of 120,000 yuan. Project 3 is a same-year product available at the beginning of Year 2; it matures at the end of Year 2 and pays 1.60 I, with an investment cap of 150,000 yuan. Project 4 is a 1-year product available at the beginning of Year 3; it matures at the end of Year 3 and pays 1.40 I, with an investment cap of 100,000 yuan. Decisions are made at the beginnings of Years 1-3 using only currently available cash. Find the investment plan that maximizes total principal plus interest at the end of Year 3.
