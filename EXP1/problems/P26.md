# P26 — `university_computer_lab_weekly_staff_scheduling`

## Full problem description

A university computer lab hires 4 undergraduates (1-4) and 2 graduate students (5-6) for duty answering questions. Maximum duty hours and hourly wages are given by student and weekday. The lab is open from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM Monday-Friday, so exactly one student must be on duty during the 14 open hours each day. Each undergraduate must work at least 8 hours per week, and each graduate student at least 7 hours per week. Each student can work no more than 2 shifts per week, and no more than 3 students can be scheduled for duty each day. Minimize gross pay. Table: student 1 wage 10.0, availability Mon 6 Tue 0 Wed 6 Thu 0 Fri 7; student 2 wage 10.0, 0,8,9,6,0; student 3 wage 9.9, 4,8,3,0,5; student 4 wage 9.8, 5,5,6,0,4; student 5 wage 10.8, 3,0,5,8,0; student 6 wage 11.3, 0,6,0,6,5.
