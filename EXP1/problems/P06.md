# P06 — `china_railroad_car_relocation`

## Full problem description

The China Railroad Ministry is planning relocations of freight cars among 5 regions to prepare for the fall harvest. The cost to move one car between regions is: region 1 to regions 2,3,4,5 costs 10,12,17,34; region 2 to regions 1,3,4,5 costs 10,18,8,46; region 3 to regions 1,2,4,5 costs 12,18,9,27; region 4 to regions 1,2,3,5 costs 17,8,9,20; region 5 to regions 1,2,3,4 costs 34,46,27,20. The current number of cars and the number needed in each region: region 1 has 115 cars and needs 300; region 2 has 385 and needs 400; region 3 has 410 and needs 800; region 4 has 480 and needs 200; region 5 has 610 and needs 300. Minimize the total cost of moving cars so that every region ends with at least the number of cars it needs. Round to the nearest dollar.
